The product

One engine for equity, fixed income and portfolio attribution.

Configured and reported consistently across asset classes, engineered to reproduce your investment process precisely.

01 · Fixed income

Fixed income attribution

The platform provides the most extensive library of attribution models available from any vendor. It enables users to reproduce any investment process faithfully, with detailed reporting on a manager’s strengths and weaknesses.

The OpenPricing API lets users define additional sources of risk, so the system remains adaptable as fixed income markets evolve rather than constrained by a single vendor’s methodology.

02 · Equity & hybrid

Equity and hybrid attribution

Flametree supports Brinson-based equity attribution models, including asset allocation at multiple hierarchical levels.

Hybrid attribution combines top-down asset allocation analysis with bottom-up, security-level return decomposition, using our proprietary methodology.

03 · Open risk

OpenRisk

There is no practical limit on the number of risk functions that can be associated with any single security. An option, for example, can attribute return across delta, gamma, theta, vega and further sensitivities simply by naming the relevant risk functions on its security master record.

OpenRisk permits volatility to be derived directly from the underlying return series rather than supplied separately.

The framework is forward-compatible: a new risk class is included by adding a risk function, or by requesting one from us.
