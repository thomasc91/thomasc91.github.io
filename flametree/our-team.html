About us

Our Team

The people building Flametree’s advanced attribution and risk tools.

Andrew Colin PhD

Founder & CEO

Andrew Colin is a leading expert in the field of investment performance attribution. He has worked at Citigroup, the Commonwealth Bank of Australia, Zurich Investment Management, JP Morgan, StatPro and Queensland University of Technology, and has managed many consulting projects in defence and machine intelligence.

He holds a PhD in Mathematics from the University of St Andrews, is a Fellow of the Institute of Mathematics and its Applications, and holds Chartered Mathematician accreditation. Andrew has also held adjunct professorships at the Faculties of Business at QUT and the University of Tasmania.

Andrew is cofounder of Flametree Technologies, a company that builds advanced attribution and risk tools.

Graeme Condie

Co-Founder

Graeme Condie has been involved in the performance and attribution industry for over 30 years. Before establishing Flametree he was a director at StatPro responsible for sales and relationship management. In addition, he was instrumental in designing and delivering on the business product and acquisition strategy.

Prior to StatPro he was Executive Director at The WM Company and lead sales and relationship management across EMEA and Asia.

Graeme has been a key contributor to the development of the performance and attribution industry and uses this experience to help develop the key business strategies for Flametree.

Dan Griffiths

Strategic Adviser

Dan Griffiths is a leading consultant in performance measurement, attribution and risk with over 20 years experience in the buy-side. His client base covers asset managers, asset owners, vendors and consultancies including Allianz Global Investors, Abrdn, Alpha FMC, BNP, Moody’s, Schroders, JP Morgan, Nomura, JHI, Phoenix Group, ABN Amro, Architas / Axa, Old Mutual, Octopus Investments, Jupiter AM, TPI, ING, Pioneer / Armundi and DB.

He has delivered numerous implementations across all asset classes for both middle and front office. He also advises on M&A and PE deals in a SME capacity in fintech space (trading systems / OMS, risk engines, fund distribution platforms).

Dan provides consultancy expertise to help support the delivery of key business strategies for Flametree Technologies.
